## About

I recently completed my Master’s degree in Complex Systems, specialising in statistical physics and computational modelling of collective dynamics on networks. My thesis introduced degree‑dependent imitation and prestige biases into a social learning model, revealing how network topology shapes opinion diversity, hub influence, and steady‑state variance.

I am actively seeking a PhD position where I can combine network science, stochastic modelling, and machine learning to address fundamental problems in biophysics, neuroscience, or social dynamics.

## Selected Projects

### Impact of Vertex Degree on Exploration–Imitation Dynamics  
*M.Sc. Thesis, 2024–2025*  
Extended a social learning model with degree‑biased imitation and neighbour selection. Simulated four model variants across eight synthetic topologies (ER, WS, BA, modular). Demonstrated power‑law decay of hub influence in BA networks and topology‑sensitive steady‑state variance.  
[![Code](https://img.shields.io/badge/Code-View%20on%20GitHub-lightgrey?style=flat-square&logo=github)](https://github.com/tabatabaeiphys/Masters_thesis)

### Entropy‑Based Analysis of Centrality Distributions  
*2025*  
Quantified structural heterogeneity in real and synthetic networks using Shannon entropy of centrality distributions. Introduced joint entropy of centrality correlations as a higher‑order complexity measure.  
[![Code](https://img.shields.io/badge/Code-View%20on%20GitHub-lightgrey?style=flat-square&logo=github)](https://github.com/tabatabaeiphys/Centralityjournal)

### Modeling Enzyme Network Structure via Centrality Correlations  
*2025*  
Compared correlation matrices of seven centrality measures in 10 enzyme networks against classical generative models. Found that the Watts–Strogatz model best reproduces enzyme network architecture.  
[![Code](https://img.shields.io/badge/Code-View%20on%20GitHub-lightgrey?style=flat-square&logo=github)](https://github.com/tabatabaeiphys/Centralityjournal)

### Computational Physics in Drug Discovery  
*B.Sc. Project, 2021*  
Comprehensive review of classical and quantum computational methods in drug design (SBDD, QM/MM).
